Limits

  • Local NEAR sandbox only. Nothing ran on testnet or mainnet.
  • The guard must pay in NEP-141 tokens (ft_transfer); balances are read from the token contract.
  • Where the tokens went: Arena reads the guard's token balance before and after each attack, and every token that left it must be found at the five receivers, the agent, the outsider, the owner or an account the attack names. Tokens at the agent, the outsider or a named account count as paid beyond the rules; tokens Arena cannot find make the attempt not finished, never held. Plain NEAR the agent or the outsider gains (net of its own gas) also makes the attempt not finished.
  • Arena finds money only at accounts it knows: the receivers, the agent, the outsider, the owner, the token contract (token guards) and accounts named in the attack. Money sent anywhere else is not traced to its destination; it shows as money Arena cannot find, and the check is incomplete.
  • These are the library's attacks, not every possible attack.
  • "Held" means it held these attacks on a local sandbox. It is not a proof.
  • Races are tested with the calls sent at the same moment; timing on a real network can differ.
