Limits

  • Local NEAR sandbox only. Nothing ran on testnet or mainnet.
  • This guard pays in native NEAR. Arena reads each receiver's NEAR balance at the end of setup and after the attack; 1 Arena unit is 0.001 NEAR. Receivers sign nothing, so gas never touches them. An attempt where a receiver signed something, a receiver's balance went down, or a change was not a whole number of milliNEAR is marked as not finished, never held.
  • Where the NEAR went: Arena also reads the guard's, the agent's, the outsider's and the owner's NEAR, and any account an attack names. NEAR at the agent, the outsider or a named account counts as paid beyond the rules. The agent's and the outsider's own gas and deposits are added back first, from their transactions' receipts. More than 1 milliNEAR that Arena cannot find makes the attempt not finished, never held. The guard's own gas rewards (at most 3/10 of the gas burnt in its receipts) can hide a small outflow; that bound is in each attempt's log.
  • Attacks that work only on the NEP-141 test token (for example ft_transfer) are not part of a native guard's suite.
  • Arena finds money only at accounts it knows: the receivers, the agent, the outsider, the owner, the token contract (token guards) and accounts named in the attack. Money sent anywhere else is not traced to its destination; it shows as money Arena cannot find, and the check is incomplete.
  • These are the library's attacks, not every possible attack.
  • "Held" means it held these attacks on a local sandbox. It is not a proof.
  • Races are tested with the calls sent at the same moment; timing on a real network can differ.
