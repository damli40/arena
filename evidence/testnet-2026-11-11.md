# Testnet race with the fixed guard build (Nov 11 2026)

**Answer: the same split as Nov 9, with the new build.** On NEAR testnet, fresh accounts every trial:

| Mode | Trials where both `pay` calls reached the chain and overlapped | Receiver got (per trial) | Rule held | Pre-registered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vulnerable_fixture` | 5 of 5 | 200, 200, 200, 200, 200 | 0 of 5 | 200 in 5 of 5: **as planned** |
| `fixed` | 5 of 5 | 100, 100, 100, 100, 100 | 5 of 5 | 100 in 5 of 5: **as planned** |

**Rule tested:** an invoice may pay at most its amount (100 test tokens).

In plain words: two `pay` requests for one 100-token bill were sent at the same moment. The guard with the
planted flaw let both through and the receiver got 200 every time. The fixed guard let one through and
refused the other with `payment already in flight` every time. Plan, written before any trial:
`testnet-plan-2026-11-11.md` (with one amendment after trial 1, also written before resuming).

- **Method:** the same as Nov 9 (`testnet-2026-11-09.md`, "Method"): five fresh accounts per trial, two keys on
  the agent, both `pay` calls broadcast together (send gap 0 ms in all 10 trials), `max_gas()` (1,000 TGas),
  RPC `rpc.testnet.fastnear.com`, agent key nonces up by exactly 2 per trial.
- **Differences from Nov 9:** a new funder with a random key (below); each trial's accounts are deleted after

- **Tool:** `tools/testnet/` (`cargo run --release -- run <trials> <out.json> <funder> <tag> <mode>`).
